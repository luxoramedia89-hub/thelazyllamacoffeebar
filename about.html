Our Story

The Lazy Llama Journey

The Lazy Llama Coffee Bar was born from a love for exceptional coffee and a desire to create a space where people can slow down and savor life's moments. Founded in New York City, our journey began with a simple belief: great coffee should be accessible, enjoyable, and shared.

Our name comes from the gentle, laid-back nature of the llama—a creature that embodies the spirit of taking things slow and appreciating the simple pleasures. With locations in New York and Alaçatı, Türkiye, we've grown into a community of coffee lovers who share our passion for quality and connection.

Every cup we serve tells a story—of the farmers who grow our beans, the roasters who perfect our blends, and the baristas who craft each drink with care. We invite you to be part of our story.

Our Mission

To create exceptional coffee experiences that bring people together, support sustainable farming, and celebrate the art of slow living.

Our Values

Quality, sustainability, community, and the art of slowing down to appreciate life's moments.

Quality

Our Commitment to Quality

Sourcing

We work directly with farmers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability. Our beans are ethically sourced from the world's best growing regions.

Roasting

Small-batch roasting allows us to highlight the unique characteristics of each bean. Every batch is carefully monitored to achieve the perfect roast profile.

Freshness

We roast to order and ship within 48 hours to ensure you receive the freshest coffee possible. Always roasted, never stale.

Craftsmanship

Our baristas are trained to craft each drink with precision and care, ensuring every cup meets our high standards of excellence.

Community

Building a Coffee Community

At The Lazy Llama Coffee Bar, we believe coffee is more than a beverage—it's a way to connect. Through our events, workshops, and community initiatives, we bring people together to share their love for coffee and create lasting connections.
