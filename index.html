Our Story
Our Story

From New York to Alaçatı

The Lazy Llama Coffee Bar began with a simple idea: create a space where coffee is more than just a drink—it's an experience. With locations in the heart of New York and the charming streets of Alaçatı, Türkiye, we bring together the best of both worlds.

Our coffee is carefully sourced from the finest regions, roasted to perfection, and brewed with love. Every cup tells a story of quality, craftsmanship, and community.
