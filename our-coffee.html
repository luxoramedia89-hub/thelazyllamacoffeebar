Brew Guide

How to Brew the Perfect Cup

Learn the art of brewing coffee like a professional

Pour Over

For a clean, bright cup that highlights delicate flavors

  • Use 15g coffee per 250ml water
  • Water temperature: 92-96°C
  • Bloom for 30 seconds
  • Total brew time: 2-3 minutes

French Press

For a full-bodied, rich cup with robust flavors

  • Use 15g coffee per 250ml water
  • Water temperature: 90-95°C
  • Steep for 4 minutes
  • Press and serve immediately

Espresso

For a concentrated, intense shot of coffee

  • Use 18g coffee for double shot
  • Extraction time: 25-30 seconds
  • Yield: 36-40ml
  • Best with freshly ground beans
